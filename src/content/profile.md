## Flagship Case Studies

### 1. Enterprise Financial Reconciliation & AI Document Parsing (Matchmade)
* **Role**: Product Manager (2024 -- Present)
* **Domain**: B2B Enterprise SaaS · Financial Technology
* **The Challenge**: Corporate accounting and operations teams were burdened with a 5-day manual reconciliation cycle, comparing hundreds of thousands of ledger rows, banking statements, and corporate invoices with high error rates.
* **Product Strategy & Delivery**:
  - Designed an end-to-end multi-table automated matching engine with custom reconciliation logic.
  - Delivered operational dashboards, multi-tenant RBAC data views, and enterprise API integrations.
  - Engineered an AI proof-of-concept utilizing Docling for automated PDF-to-structured-table extraction, eliminating manual document re-keying.
* **Measurable Impact**:
  - **IDR 1.2 Billion/year** cost savings per enterprise client.
  - **80% reduction** in reconciliation cycle duration (5 days down to 1 day).
  - Scaled transaction processing throughput from **11 GB/month to 34 GB/month** (3x growth).

### 2. High-Volume Consumer Marketplace & Payment Modernization (PegiPegi)
* **Role**: Product Manager (2022 -- 2023)
* **Domain**: B2C OTA (Accommodations, Flights, Transport)
* **The Challenge**: Two years of payment channel stagnation, high customer support ticket volumes on un-settled transactions, and escalating product messaging expenses.
* **Product Strategy & Delivery**:
  - Unlocked new customer acquisition cohorts by integrating modern payment gateways after 2 years of inactivity.
  - Engineered customer deduplication algorithms to eliminate redundant accounts.
  - Introduced WhatsApp transactional notification infrastructure and streamlined internal customer service tooling.
* **Measurable Impact**:
  - Cut **~85% of time** for non-settled card refund processing.
  - Reduced redundant accounts by **~50%**, saving **50% of monthly product messaging quota**.
  - Lowered payment checkout duration by **~40%** and drove **~10% new transacting users**.
  - Slashed **6% of communication costs** with WhatsApp alerts achieving a **75% open rate**.

### 3. Recruitment Marketplace Funnel Optimization (AdaKerja)
* **Role**: Product Manager (2021 -- 2022)
* **Domain**: B2C Job Platform & Blue-Collar Marketplace
* **The Challenge**: Low conversion in mobile payment funnels and fragmented squad workflows during corporate restructuring.
* **Product Strategy & Delivery**:
  - Established a unified product roadmap aligning 3 engineering squads with business leadership.
  - Streamlined candidate application funnels and redesigned mobile checkout touchpoints.
* **Measurable Impact**:
  - Increased payment click-through rate (**CTR**) by **~60%**.
  - Created core product architectures driving scalable active job-seeker engagement.
